Carmona OS Support

Help with your account, workouts, saved progress, or the app.

Contact us

Email support@carmonaos.com.

Include the email associated with your account, your device model, and a short description of what happened. Do not send passwords or Apple verification codes.

Common support topics

  • Signing in or restoring progress
  • Workout logging, exercise swaps, and next-weight guidance
  • 45 Min and Full workout modes
  • Nutrition guidance
  • Account privacy or deletion

Delete your account

In Carmona OS, open Profile and choose Delete Account. The app will ask you to confirm before permanently deleting your profile and saved workouts.

Privacy requests

For a copy, correction, or deletion request involving your personal data, email privacy@carmonaos.com.
